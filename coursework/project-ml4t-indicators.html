Indicator Evaluation

Coursework / ML4T / Indicator Evaluation

Problem

Implement five technical indicators and establish the performance ceiling any strategy could reach on the same symbol.

Approach

  • Momentum, Price/SMA ratio, RSI, Stochastic and MACD, each built from scratch
  • A Theoretically Optimal Strategy constructed with perfect foresight, buying local minima and selling local maxima
  • That ceiling used as the reference every later strategy is measured against
  • Indicator periods treated as hyperparameters to be tuned, not as universal constants

Results

Indicator Period Signal Type
Momentum 12 days Crosses through zero
Price/SMA Ratio 20 days Above/below threshold (1.05/0.95)
RSI 14 days Overbought (>70), Oversold (<30)
Stochastic 14 days %K/%D crossovers
MACD 12 fast / 26 slow Histogram + signal line crossover
Strategy Cumulative Return Sharpe Ratio
Buy & Hold Benchmark 1.23% 0.157
Theoretically Optimal Strategy 578.6% 13.32

No indicator is reliable alone, and the ceiling matters because it answers the only question worth asking: how much of the available alpha a strategy actually captures.
