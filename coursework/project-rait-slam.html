Indiana Drones (SLAM)

Coursework / RAIT / Indiana Drones (SLAM)

Problem

A drone enters an unmapped area, flies a prescripted route, and must estimate both its own position and the position of every tree from noisy range and bearing readings. The second half navigates it to a target and extracts it without striking a canopy.

Approach

  • GraphSLAM: every movement and every sighting accumulated as a constraint in one linear system, solved for drone and landmarks together
  • Re-observing a known landmark tightens earlier trajectory estimates, not just the current pose
  • Landmarks enter and leave sensor range, which is precisely what makes re-observation worth exploiting
  • Navigation tests the swept segment against each canopy rather than the movement endpoints
  • On a predicted collision the heading is deflected or the step shortened until a safe move is found

GraphSLAM assumes independent Gaussian noise in x and y while the real noise lives in range and bearing; heading was carried forward from commanded turns, the weakest part of the build.
