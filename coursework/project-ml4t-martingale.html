Martingale Simulator

Coursework / ML4T / Martingale Simulator

Problem

Quantify the Martingale betting strategy on American roulette under an idealized unlimited bankroll against a realistic capped one.

Approach

  • Monte Carlo across 1,000 independent episodes of 1,000 spins, rather than a single run
  • Aggregate statistics collected over the full distribution of outcomes
  • Running means and standard deviations computed during the sweep
  • Bet doubled after each loss and reset after each win, against fixed 18/38 odds

Results

1,000 episodes
1M total spins
18/38 win probability
Metric Unlimited Bankroll Limited ($256)
Win rate (reaching +$80) 100% 66%
Expected value +$80.00 -$33.73
Median outcome $80 $80
Outcome at worst no bankruptcy -$256 (34%)

Median and expected value diverge sharply here: “I usually win” conceals a tail catastrophic enough to make the whole strategy unprofitable.
