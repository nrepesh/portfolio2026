// CS 7646

Machine Learning for Trading

Spring 2026 · Georgia Tech OMSCS · All classes

Project-driven course on applying machine learning to financial market data. Covers manipulating time-series data with NumPy / Pandas, computing technical indicators, portfolio optimization with Sharpe ratio, training decision-tree and random-forest learners, building a market simulator from scratch, and applying Q-learning to trading strategies.
